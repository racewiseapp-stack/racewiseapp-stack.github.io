In short: RaceWise has no analytics. Your training data stays on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud; the developer never receives it. RaceWise's server is used only if you connect Garmin (it keeps only what's needed to link your accounts, see Garmin below), if you send feedback, and for crash reports, which you can turn off (see Feedback and crash reports below).

What RaceWise keeps, and where

  • On your iPhone: your races, plan, workouts (logged by you, or imported from Apple Health with their date, distance, duration and average and maximum heart rate), check-ins (tiredness, soreness, pain), training days, training method and profile (for example age, which you enter or RaceWise works out from your date of birth, injury history, and maximum and resting heart rate, which you enter or RaceWise detects from Apple Health).
  • In your private iCloud: these items sync to your other devices through Apple's iCloud, in a private database that only your Apple Account can read. Heart-rate information is not synced: heart rates of workouts imported from Apple Health, your maximum and resting heart rate, your heart-rate zones, and daily resting heart rate, HRV and sleep readings, along with any warnings worked out from them, stay on the iPhone that read them. Each of your devices uses its own. The sessions they made easier do sync, as part of your plan. Heart rates you type into a workout yourself sync with that workout. If iCloud isn't available, everything stays on your iPhone.

Optional details kept only on your iPhone

You can choose to tell RaceWise your sex and (in the daily check-in) whether you have period symptoms today. These are optional and are kept only on your iPhone, not in iCloud and never in your plan. They are used only to estimate your maximum heart rate more closely and to make a day's session easier, never harder. RaceWise doesn't guide training during pregnancy or after birth: please talk to your doctor or midwife. Period-symptom days are kept for two weeks. Deleting all data in the app removes them.

Apple Health

With your permission RaceWise reads workouts (runs, swims, rides, rows, hikes) with their distance, time, climb and heart rate, plus resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep and date of birth, to set up and adjust your plan. It does not write anything to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising and never shared with third parties. If you turn off access in Settings, RaceWise stops reading new data; what was already imported stays until you delete it.

Updates while RaceWise is closed: when a new workout arrives in Apple Health, and once each morning (when iOS allows), RaceWise reads Apple Health on your iPhone and updates your plan. If that changes today's or tomorrow's session, it can show a notification on your iPhone. This all happens on your iPhone; nothing is sent to RaceWise's server. You can turn these notifications off in Settings › Notifications.

Routes: only if you turn on Route when sharing a workout, RaceWise asks to read that workout's route from Apple Health and draws its shape on the share image, with no map and without the first and last 200 m. The route is used only to draw that image; RaceWise doesn't save it, sync it or send it anywhere.

RaceWise gives general training guidance. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or rule out any condition.

Location

Only during iPhone voice guidance, and only with your permission, RaceWise uses your location to measure distance and pace. The route isn't saved; only the session's distance and time are kept, like any other workout.

Weather is off until you turn it on. Then RaceWise rounds your location to about 10 km and asks Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for the forecast; if a race or hike has a city, that city's name is used to find its forecast. RaceWise doesn't save your location or the forecast, and neither goes to RaceWise's server. Apple handles weather requests under its own privacy policy. You can turn weather off in Settings › Weather.

Routes for a session: only when you tap Route on a session, and with your permission, RaceWise uses your precise location to find walking routes from where you are, using Apple Maps (MapKit). Your location and the route's turning points go to Apple to work out the routes. If you choose to follow a route in Google Maps, its start and turning points are passed to Google Maps, which handles them under Google's privacy policy. RaceWise doesn't save your location or the routes, and neither goes to RaceWise's server.

Apple Watch

If you turn it on, RaceWise places your upcoming sessions in the Workout app on your Apple Watch using Apple's WorkoutKit. Finished workouts come back through Apple Health.

Garmin (optional)

If you choose to connect Garmin, you sign in with Apple once and allow RaceWise in your Garmin Connect account. RaceWise then sends your upcoming sessions to Garmin Connect, so they appear in your Garmin calendar and on your Garmin watch, and brings your finished Garmin runs, rides, swims and rows into RaceWise, so your plan adjusts.

For this, RaceWise uses a small server of its own, hosted by Cloudflare. It keeps only: the private ID that Sign in with Apple gives RaceWise (not your name or email), your Garmin user ID, and the keys Garmin issues for your account, encrypted. Your sessions pass through the server on their way to Garmin and aren't kept there. When you finish an activity, Garmin sends the server its summary: type, date and start time, duration, distance, average and maximum heart rate, metres climbed (for hikes), and the device name (no route or location). The server keeps it only until your iPhone collects it, and for at most 7 days. On your iPhone it is saved like your other workouts: the summary syncs through your private iCloud, while its heart rates stay on that iPhone. Your plan, races and check-ins are never stored on the server.

RaceWise reads only these activity summaries, not your other Garmin data (such as sleep, stress or daily steps). Workouts from a Garmin device are labelled with the device's name wherever RaceWise shows them, including images you share. Garmin data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never processed by any AI system or used to train AI; the on-device coach reads your plan, not your Garmin activities. The third parties involved are Garmin (receives your sessions and sends your activity summaries), Cloudflare (hosts the server and processes the items above on RaceWise's behalf) and Apple (Sign in with Apple).

You can disconnect at any time in RaceWise (Settings › Garmin › Disconnect Garmin) or in Garmin Connect. Disconnecting deletes the link, the Garmin keys and any activities not yet collected from the server, and tells Garmin. Workouts already saved in RaceWise stay until you delete them. Sessions already in your Garmin calendar stay until you delete them in Garmin Connect. To see, correct or get a copy of what the server keeps about you, email racewiseapp@gmail.com.

AI coach

Coaching text is written by Apple Intelligence on your device. Anything you type for RaceWise to read is read there too: a check-in note, how a session felt, a request to change your plan, a question about your plan, or a description of your training. What you type isn't saved, only the values and changes you confirm. Nothing you type or any of your data is sent to an AI service. Without Apple Intelligence, RaceWise uses built-in wording.

Photos

If you choose a photo to read a result from (for example a finisher certificate or a treadmill screen), RaceWise reads the text on your iPhone. The photo isn't saved or uploaded; only the numbers you check and save are kept. RaceWise can see only the photo you pick. If you share a workout card and choose to save it, RaceWise asks for permission to add that image to your Photos; it can't read your library.

Siri and Shortcuts

You can ask Siri for today's training or your race countdown, log a workout, check in or change your plan. Siri handles your voice under Apple's own privacy terms; RaceWise receives only the request and does the rest on your iPhone. Siri asks you to unlock your iPhone first, and reads changes back to you to confirm before they are made.

Event list

RaceWise comes with a built-in list of events. To keep it up to date, the app may download a public list of events from this website, at most once a day, when you open the event list. The request carries no personal information. Like any website, the host may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses.

Feedback and crash reports

If you send feedback from Settings › Send feedback, RaceWise's server receives your note, the email address if you add one (only so we can reply), and the app version, iOS version, iPhone model and language. Nothing from your plan, workouts or Apple Health is sent. Feedback is kept for up to a year, and you can ask for it to be deleted by emailing racewiseapp@gmail.com.

If RaceWise crashes or freezes, Apple's diagnostic report on it (MetricKit: what the app was doing when it failed, the iOS version and iPhone model) is sent to the server, with the app version and language, so we can fix the problem. It has no name, account, location or training data, and it's kept for 90 days. You can turn this off in Settings › Send crash reports. Both go to the same Cloudflare-hosted server as Garmin, and neither is used for advertising or analytics.

What RaceWise doesn't do

  • No account with us unless you connect Garmin, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling of data, and no sharing beyond what you turn on (Apple Watch, Garmin) or send (feedback, crash reports).
  • If you add a RaceWise widget, it shows your upcoming sessions and race countdown on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, where anyone holding your iPhone can see them. The widget reads a small file that only RaceWise can write; it is removed when you delete your data.
  • Reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone.

Beta testing

If you test through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

How long data is kept

RaceWise keeps your data until you delete it. Earlier versions of your plan are kept as history. Daily recovery readings cover the last 30 days and are replaced at each sync. Your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer) may include RaceWise's data on that iPhone, depending on your settings.

On Android

RaceWise for Android works the same way, with these differences:

  • Health Connect takes the place of Apple Health. With your permission RaceWise reads the same kinds of data (workouts with distance, time, climb and heart rate, plus resting heart rate, heart-rate variability and sleep). It is read-only: RaceWise never writes to Health Connect, and what it reads stays on your phone.
  • Backup: instead of iCloud, Android’s own backup (if you have it turned on) copies your plans, workouts and settings to your private Google Drive backup, encrypted. Health readings from Health Connect are never included; after a restore, RaceWise reads them from Health Connect again.
  • Export: a file with your RaceWise data is only made when you choose Settings › Export data, and it’s saved where you choose.
  • Garmin uses Google sign-in instead of Sign in with Apple; the server keeps the same items as above, with a private Google ID instead of an Apple one.

Deleting your data

In RaceWise, Settings › Delete all data removes your races, plans, workouts, check-ins and profile from your iPhone and, through iCloud, from your other devices. It also removes RaceWise reminders and sessions sent to your Apple Watch, and stops reading Apple Health. Apple Health itself isn't changed. If your iPhone is offline, the deletion reaches iCloud when it's back online; if iCloud isn't available, RaceWise tells you and asks you to finish deleting later. If you connected Garmin, it also deletes your account and Garmin link from RaceWise's server and tells Garmin.

Deleting the app removes its data from that iPhone only, not the copy in your iCloud. You can also revoke Health, location and Apple Watch access at any time in Settings.

On Android, Settings › Delete all data works the same way on your phone (there’s no iCloud copy). Step by step, including how to delete your account without the app: Delete your account or data.
