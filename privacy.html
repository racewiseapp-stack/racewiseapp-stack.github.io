Privacy policy

Racewise中文

Effective 18 October 2026

In short: Racewise has no server and no analytics. The developer never receives your data. Everything stays on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud.

What Racewise keeps, and where

  • On your iPhone: your races, plan, logged workouts, check-ins (tiredness, soreness, pain), training days and profile (for example age, injury history, heart rates you enter).
  • In your private iCloud: the same items sync to your other devices through Apple's iCloud, in a private database that only your Apple Account can read. Daily resting heart rate, HRV and sleep readings are not synced.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional): if you use it, the name and email Apple shares are kept the same way, on your devices and in your private iCloud only.

Apple Health

With your permission Racewise reads workouts (runs, swims, rides, rows) and their heart rate, plus resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep and date of birth, to set up and adjust your plan. It does not write anything to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising and never shared.

Location

Only during iPhone voice guidance, and only with your permission, Racewise uses your location to measure distance and pace. The route isn't saved; only the session's distance and time are kept, like any other workout.

Apple Watch

If you turn it on, Racewise places your upcoming sessions in the Workout app on your Apple Watch using Apple's WorkoutKit. Finished workouts come back through Apple Health.

AI coach

Coaching text is written by Apple Intelligence on your device. Nothing you type or any of your data is sent to an AI service.

Event list

To show up-to-date races, the app downloads a public list of events from this website. The request carries no personal information. Like any website, the host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses.

What Racewise doesn't do

  • No account with us, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling or sharing of data.
  • Reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone.

Beta testing

If you test through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove data from your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Racewise › Delete Data. You can also revoke Health, location and Apple Watch access at any time in Settings.

Contact

Questions: racewiseapp@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
