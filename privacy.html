In short: RaceWise has no server and no analytics. The developer never receives your data. Everything stays on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud.

What RaceWise keeps, and where

  • On your iPhone: your races, plan, workouts (logged by you, or imported from Apple Health with their date, distance, duration and average and maximum heart rate), check-ins (tiredness, soreness, pain), training days, training method and profile (for example age, which you enter or RaceWise works out from your date of birth, injury history, and maximum and resting heart rate, which you enter or RaceWise detects from Apple Health).
  • In your private iCloud: these items sync to your other devices through Apple's iCloud, in a private database that only your Apple Account can read. Heart-rate information is not synced: heart rates of workouts imported from Apple Health, your maximum and resting heart rate, your heart-rate zones, and daily resting heart rate, HRV and sleep readings, along with any warnings worked out from them, stay on the iPhone that read them. Each of your devices uses its own. The sessions they made easier do sync, as part of your plan. Heart rates you type into a workout yourself sync with that workout. If iCloud isn't available, everything stays on your iPhone.

Optional details kept only on your iPhone

You can choose to tell RaceWise your sex and (in the daily check-in) whether you have period symptoms today. These are optional and are kept only on your iPhone, not in iCloud and never in your plan. They are used only to estimate your maximum heart rate more closely and to make a day's session easier, never harder. RaceWise doesn't guide training during pregnancy or after birth: please talk to your doctor or midwife. Period-symptom days are kept for two weeks. Deleting all data in the app removes them.

Apple Health

With your permission RaceWise reads workouts (runs, swims, rides, rows) and their heart rate, plus resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep and date of birth, to set up and adjust your plan. It does not write anything to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising and never shared with third parties. If you turn off access in Settings, RaceWise stops reading new data; what was already imported stays until you delete it.

RaceWise gives general training guidance. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or rule out any condition.

Location

Only during iPhone voice guidance, and only with your permission, RaceWise uses your location to measure distance and pace. The route isn't saved; only the session's distance and time are kept, like any other workout.

Apple Watch

If you turn it on, RaceWise places your upcoming sessions in the Workout app on your Apple Watch using Apple's WorkoutKit. Finished workouts come back through Apple Health.

AI coach

Coaching text is written by Apple Intelligence on your device. Anything you type for RaceWise to read is read there too: a check-in note, how a session felt, a request to change your plan, a question about your plan, or a description of your training. What you type isn't saved, only the values and changes you confirm. Nothing you type or any of your data is sent to an AI service. Without Apple Intelligence, RaceWise uses built-in wording.

Photos

If you choose a photo to read a result from (for example a finisher certificate or a treadmill screen), RaceWise reads the text on your iPhone. The photo isn't saved or uploaded; only the numbers you check and save are kept. RaceWise can see only the photo you pick. If you share a workout card and choose to save it, RaceWise asks for permission to add that image to your Photos; it can't read your library.

Siri and Shortcuts

You can ask Siri for today's training or your race countdown, log a workout, check in or change your plan. Siri handles your voice under Apple's own privacy terms; RaceWise receives only the request and does the rest on your iPhone. Siri asks you to unlock your iPhone first, and reads changes back to you to confirm before they are made.

Event list

RaceWise comes with a built-in list of events. To keep it up to date, the app may download a public list of events from this website, at most once a day, when you open the event list. The request carries no personal information. Like any website, the host may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses.

What RaceWise doesn't do

  • No account with us, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling or sharing of data.
  • If you add a RaceWise widget, it shows your upcoming sessions and race countdown on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, where anyone holding your iPhone can see them. The widget reads a small file that only RaceWise can write; it is removed when you delete your data.
  • Reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone.

Beta testing

If you test through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

How long data is kept

RaceWise keeps your data until you delete it. Earlier versions of your plan are kept as history. Daily recovery readings cover the last 30 days and are replaced at each sync. Your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer) may include RaceWise's data on that iPhone, depending on your settings.

Deleting your data

In RaceWise, Settings › Delete all data removes your races, plans, workouts, check-ins and profile from your iPhone and, through iCloud, from your other devices. It also removes RaceWise reminders and sessions sent to your Apple Watch, and stops reading Apple Health. Apple Health itself isn't changed. If your iPhone is offline, the deletion reaches iCloud when it's back online; if iCloud isn't available, RaceWise tells you and asks you to finish deleting later.

Deleting the app removes its data from that iPhone only, not the copy in your iCloud. You can also revoke Health, location and Apple Watch access at any time in Settings.
