About RaceWise

RaceWise is an iPhone app that builds a safe, gradual training plan for a race, a personal goal or a new habit, and keeps adjusting it as you train. The free version is a full plan, not a trial: one sport, every safety check and Apple Watch sessions, with no time limit.

RaceWise Pro, an optional subscription, plans running, swimming, cycling, rowing and hiking together in the same week, and adds Garmin Connect two-way sync, the full race-day plan, routes, Lock Screen and widgets, and the exercise timer.

  • Adjusts to how you feel: resting heart rate, HRV and sleep from Apple Health, and a daily check-in, can only make the plan easier
  • Safety rules: weekly load grows gently, with lower limits for beginners and over-60s; hard days never fall back to back across sports
  • Hilly races and big hikes: the plan adds hill repeats, hilly long runs and downhill practice
  • Coach uses Apple Intelligence on the iPhone to explain the plan; it never changes a number
  • Private: no ads, no analytics, no account needed; plans sync only through the user’s own iCloud
  • Garmin Connect: sessions go to the Garmin calendar and finished activities come back (Pro)
