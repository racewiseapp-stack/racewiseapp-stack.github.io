RaceWisePrivacy policy

RaceWise, a training-plan app for iPhone and Android, published on Google Play by BOM Kind Notes. Updated 30 October 2026.

In short: RaceWise doesn’t have accounts of its own, and your training data is kept on your phone, not on our server. You can delete everything from inside the app, or ask us by email.

Delete your account

You don’t need an account to use RaceWise. The only account RaceWise keeps on its server is made if you connect Garmin: a private ID from Sign in with Apple (iPhone) or Google sign-in (Android), not your name or email, plus your Garmin link and the keys Garmin issues for it.

  • Delete the account and all your data: in RaceWise, open Settings › Delete all data. This deletes your account and Garmin link from RaceWise’s server and tells Garmin, as well as deleting your training data (see below).
  • Remove only the Garmin link: open Settings › Garmin › Disconnect Garmin. This deletes the link, the Garmin keys and any activities not yet collected from the server, and tells Garmin.
  • Without the app: email racewiseapp@gmail.com and we’ll delete your account and everything the server keeps about you.

Delete your data

Your races, plans, workouts, check-ins and profile are kept on your phone. RaceWise never sends them to its own server.

  • In the app: Settings › Delete all data removes them, along with RaceWise reminders, widgets and, if you connected Garmin, your account on the server. Apple Health and Health Connect themselves aren’t changed.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from that phone.
  • Backups: on iPhone, a copy syncs through your private iCloud; Settings › Delete all data removes that copy too. On Android, if Google backup is on, your plans, workouts and settings are included in your own Google Drive backup (health readings from Health Connect never are). You can delete that backup in your Google account settings.
  • Export files (Android) are only made when you choose to export your data in Settings, and are saved where you choose; delete them like any other file.
  • Feedback you sent from Settings › Send feedback is kept for up to a year. Email racewiseapp@gmail.com to have it deleted sooner.

Crash reports carry no name, account or training data and are deleted after 90 days. See the privacy policy for everything RaceWise keeps.
