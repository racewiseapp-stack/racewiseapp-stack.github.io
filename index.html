Safety first

Built to keep you running, not to push you.

Every plan passes a safety check before you see it. Weekly volume rises slowly (more slowly for beginners, over-50s and anyone coming back from injury), and big changes wait for your OK. Report pain and it is never played down; warning signs such as chest pain, palpitations or sharp bone pain tell you to stop and get checked.

Builds no more than 10% a weekEasier weeks to recover
