The basics

A plan that keeps up with your week.

Life gets in the way of training. RaceWise expects that.

Start where you are

Never run before? Choose My first 5K: you start with short run-walk intervals, and the app marks each milestone. Already training? Pick one of hundreds of checked events, or set your own date.

Moves with you

Move a session, skip one or do it a day early, and the rest of the week is rebuilt. Missed sessions aren’t piled onto later weeks.

Checks in each morning

RaceWise reads your sleep, resting heart rate and HRV from Apple Health. If the plan needs to change, a notice tells you what changed and why.

On your Apple Watch

Sessions appear in the Workout app with pace and heart-rate alerts, step by step.

Notices pain patterns

If pain keeps following one kind of session, RaceWise tells you and offers a gentler version of it.

Private

No ads and no analytics. The coach uses Apple Intelligence™ on your iPhone, so what you type stays there.

Coach

The coach can’t change your numbers.

Coaching notes are written on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence. Paces, distances and dates come from the training plan. If a note contains a number that isn’t in your plan, RaceWise shows a plain summary instead.

Safety

Slow, steady increases.

Every plan passes a safety check before you see it. Weekly volume rises slowly, and more slowly for beginners, older athletes and anyone coming back from injury. Long runs grow by no more than 10% at a time. Big changes wait for your OK. Chest pain, palpitations or sharp bone pain: RaceWise tells you to stop and get checked.

BaseBuildTaper

Up to 10% more a week, with lighter weeks to recover.

GarminPro

Your sessions on your Garmin.

Connect the Garmin Connect™ app and RaceWise sends the coming week’s running, cycling, swimming and strength sessions to your Garmin calendar, with pace and heart-rate targets. Finished Garmin activities come back into RaceWise, and the plan adjusts. You can disconnect at any time.

How it works

Three steps.

Tell it your goal

A race, a target date or a habit, and how training is going now. A few minutes, no account.

Get your plan

Easy days, long days and harder sessions, with paces and heart-rate zones that fit you.

Train

Log or sync each session. RaceWise looks at how you’re doing and adjusts the weeks ahead.

Your training stays yours.

No ads and no analytics. Your plan and workouts stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. A small server is used only if you connect Garmin or send feedback. Read the privacy policy.
