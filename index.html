A training plan for your race day that changes with you – for running, swimming, cycling and rowing.

Coming soon to the App Store

Built around your race
Pick your event and date. Racewise builds a safe, step-by-step plan from where you are now.
Adjusts as you go
Move or skip sessions, run a day early – the plan rebalances. It never crams missed training back in.
Listens to your body
Reads runs, resting heart rate, HRV and sleep from Apple Health and eases off when you need it.
On your Apple Watch
Sessions appear in the watch Workout app with pace and heart-rate alerts. Nothing to tap in the app.
Several sports at once
Running, swimming, cycling and rowing races in one plan; hard days never clash.
Private by design
No account needed, no ads, no tracking. The AI coach runs on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

Support

Questions or ideas? Email racewiseapp@gmail.com. In the TestFlight beta, take a screenshot in the app and choose Send Beta Feedback.
