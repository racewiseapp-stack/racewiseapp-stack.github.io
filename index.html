Safety first

Built to keep you running, not to push you.

Every plan passes a safety check before you see it. Weekly volume rises slowly (more slowly for beginners, older athletes and anyone coming back from injury), long runs grow by no more than 10% at a time, and big changes wait for your OK. Report pain and it is never played down; warning signs such as chest pain, palpitations or sharp bone pain tell you to stop and get checked.

Hiking

Train for the trail, not just the road.

Day hikes and multi-day treks join running, swimming, cycling and rowing. Enter the distance, the climb and how many days; RaceWise counts every 100 m of climb like an extra kilometre, builds your pack weight a little at a time, and adds downhill practice in the last weeks.

Garmin

Your sessions, on your Garmin.

Connect the Garmin Connect™ app and RaceWise sends the coming week’s running, cycling, swimming and strength sessions to your Garmin calendar, with pace and heart-rate targets, ready on your Garmin watch. Change your plan and the sessions on Garmin change too. Optional; you can disconnect at any time.
