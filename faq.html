Common questions

Can I use it without Apple Health or a watch?

Yes. Tell RaceWise where you are now and log sessions by hand; the plan builds from there. Apple Health only adds detail.

I wear a Garmin.

If Garmin Connect syncs with Apple Health, your Garmin workouts come into RaceWise by themselves. With Pro, the coming week’s sessions also go to your Garmin calendar, and finished Garmin activities come back.

Does RaceWise work with Fitbit?

Fitbit doesn’t save workouts to Apple Health, so RaceWise can’t see them on its own. Use a sync app that copies your Fitbit workouts into Apple Health, and RaceWise reads them from there, just as it does for Garmin, Coros, Polar and Apple Watch. If the same run turns up twice (say, from two apps), it only counts once.

Running on a treadmill?

Choose treadmill in Settings, or switch it on for a single run. You’ll see the speed in km/h next to the pace.

More than one sport: which comes first?

When two sports’ sessions would clash, races pick their days first, then personal targets, then habits, and the sooner one first. The others move to another day or become easy. All your sports together grow by at most about 10% a week too: easy sessions get shorter first.

Is it different for older athletes?

Yes, when you enter your age. From 60, the plan builds more gently with more recovery between hard days; from 65, strength sessions add balance moves.

Why did my plan change?

Check-ins, missed or extra sessions, a new race date, an app update and your watch’s recovery signs all update it. Each time, Today and the Plan tab say why and what changed.

Why no intervals?

With two days a week, in the first weeks for beginners, or in base weeks with some methods, the plan keeps you easy first. Faster work comes when it’s safe.

Why is today’s run so short?

The week’s total grows slowly, and the long run takes the biggest share. With few training days, what’s left for the other runs can be short. More training days, or a few more weeks of steady running, give each run more.

Why does my long run grow so slowly?

A run more than about 10% longer than your longest in the last month raises the risk of injury (a 2025 study of over 5,000 runners), so each long run grows by at most 10%.

I added a run, but it’s shorter than I asked for.

An extra run is at most as long as the week’s usual easy run, so one week never jumps.

How do I change a race?

Tap the race in the Plan tab to change its name, date, distance or target, make it the main race or remove it.

Why are some methods named after coaches?

Only where RaceWise uses their published formula exactly: Jack Daniels’ paces and Phil Maffetone’s 180 formula. How much you train each week is RaceWise’s own safety rules.

Where’s my data?

On your iPhone and in your private iCloud. Daily resting heart rate, HRV and sleep stay on your iPhone. RaceWise uses a server only to connect Garmin, for feedback you send and for crash reports. If you connect Garmin, your sessions pass through it on their way to Garmin and finished activities on their way back; otherwise it never gets your plan or health data. The privacy policy has the details.
